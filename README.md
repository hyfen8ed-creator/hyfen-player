# HyFen Player: Free Open-Source YouTube Work Player

A lightweight, distraction-free, ad-minimizing open-source YouTube web player designed for remote work environments, corporate networks, and clean listening.

## ✨ Features
- **Zero Ads / Bloat:** Strips out recommendation feeds, comment sections, sidebar clutter, and algorithm rabbit holes.
- **Privacy & Proxy Engines:** Toggle between `youtube-nocookie.com`, standard embed, or privacy frontend proxies (`yewtu.be`, `invidious.nerdvpn.de`) to bypass restrictive work firewalls.
- **Link Auto-Detection:** Paste full YouTube URLs, `youtu.be` short links, raw 11-character video IDs, or playlist links.
- **1-Click Work Presets:** Quick buttons for Lofi Girl, Synthwave, and Focus alpha binaural beats.
- **Popout Window Mode:** Detach the player into a small borderless floating window next to your work apps.
- **Local History & Loop:** Remembers your recently played tracks directly in your browser's `localStorage` (no accounts or tracking).
